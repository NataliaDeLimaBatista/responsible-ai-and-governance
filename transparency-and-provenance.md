# Transparency and Provenance

Resources for documenting AI systems and communicating where digital content comes from.

## Model documentation and content provenance

- [Model Cards — Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/models/model-cards/)
- [C2PA and Content Credentials](https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.4/explainer/Explainer.html)

## Suggested additions

- [Model Cards for Model Reporting](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.03993) — Mitchell et al. propose documenting the intended uses, evaluation, and performance of trained models.
- [Datasheets for Datasets](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.09010) — Gebru et al. propose documenting how datasets were created and how they are meant to be used.
